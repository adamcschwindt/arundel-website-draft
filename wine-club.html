Questions

Is there a fee to join?

No. You only pay for your wine each shipment. You can cancel anytime.

Can I choose my wines?

Yes. About a week before each shipment we email you our picks. Click the link to swap in any wines you like. If you do nothing, we send our picks. You can also switch between the Sweet and Dry clubs.

Do I have to pick up my wine?

No. You can pick up at the members' party, pick up any time during open hours, or have it shipped. Shipping costs extra.

When is my card charged?

When your shipment is ready, in February, May, August, and November. We email you before each one.

Can I give it as a gift?

Yes. A wine club membership makes a great gift. Ask at the bar or call us at 814-725-1079.

Do I have to be 21?

Yes. Members and anyone picking up or receiving wine must be 21 or older.

Ready to join?

Sign up online, or ask any of our staff on your next visit.
