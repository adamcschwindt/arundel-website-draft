Questions

Do you take reservations?

We don't take normal reservations. If you are bringing a group of 10 or more, please call us first at 814-725-1079. This helps us have enough staff, and we can suggest a time that does not overlap with other large groups.

Do you have gluten free options?

Yes. Our kitchen has gluten free crackers and bread on request. All of our wines and hard ciders are gluten free.

Are you dog friendly?

We love dogs! Dogs must stay on a leash and stay outside, on one of our patios or in the open area behind the building.

Can I buy wine to take home?

Yes. Buy bottles at the bar, or order online and we ship to you. Free shipping on 12 bottles or more.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes. Buy gift cards online or at the bar.
