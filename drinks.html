Craft Beer

What's on tap?

We have a 12-tap draft system that changes with the seasons. There is always something for beer beginners, IPA lovers, and seasonal fans.

  • Flight: four 6 oz pours of your choice
  • Pint: 16 oz glass
  • To go: 2 liter growler or 32 oz crowler can

Coming for one special beer? Some are seasonal. Call first to check: 814-725-1079.
