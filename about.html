Our Story

A family winery in a restored 1800s cider barn.

Our building

Why "Arundel"?

Our barn was built in the late 1800s and was a cider mill for many years.

In 1921, Frank Shultz bought the barn, the apple orchards and the vineyards. He had just come home from World War I. In England he had seen a beautiful castle called Arundel. He painted it on the side of his barn, with his business name, Shultz Farm. The cider mill ran for three generations.

In 2012 our family bought the farm. Like "Grandpa Shultz," we loved the castle. This barn was our castle too, so we named our business Arundel Cellars & Brewing Co.

The remodel

Bringing the barn back

We started restoring the orchards and vineyards in May 2012. Work on the barn started in December 2012. We opened our doors in 2014, and we celebrated 10 years in 2024.

Our place

Lake Erie Wine Country

We are in the largest grape growing area east of the Rocky Mountains. Lake Erie keeps our falls warm, so our grapes have more time to ripen. Our sandy soil adds flavor and drains well.

We are proud members of Lake Erie Wine Country, which promotes the wineries along the south shore of Lake Erie.

Our people

Four families, one dream team

Arundel is run by four families with a long history in wine and grape growing. Together we have over 100 years of experience.

Our goal is simple: make great wine and beer, keep the room warm and welcoming, and make every guest feel at home.
